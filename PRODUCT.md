# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PUBG Steam PC players who feel their recoil control is inconsistent and want a guided, offline way to compare sensitivity changes without reading game processes or editing game files.

## Product Purpose

PUBG 灵敏度诊断工具 turns a vague feeling such as “压不住枪” into a repeatable workflow: confirm the complete setup, run a fixed Beryl red-dot 50 m baseline, then compare one or more carefully bounded candidate settings under matching conditions. Hit placement and spread lead the comparison; hand-feel is a constraint. A recommendation is a testable candidate, never a claim of a uniquely best sensitivity.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, encouraging. The interface should feel like a coach that explains the next action, not a spreadsheet that asks the player to interpret every control.

## Anti-references

Avoid configuration-dump screens, unexplained expert terminology, aggressive “best sensitivity” claims, and forms that expose advanced variables before the user has chosen a test scenario.

## Design Principles

1. Show the next action before the full configuration.
2. Record the current complete setup as a fixed baseline before proposing numeric changes.
3. Keep advanced settings available but progressive, not competing with the main task.
4. Explain why each input matters at the point of entry.
5. Compare baseline and candidate rounds with settings snapshots, impact center/spread, and hand-feel; inconclusive or conflicting results call for repeat tests.
6. Allow linked candidate bundles in the default workflow and a guided one-variable mode when the issue is isolated; always show the exact values and manual PUBG steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear Chinese labels, high-contrast text, visible focus and disabled states, keyboard-accessible controls, and do not rely on color alone to communicate test status. Keep important instructions visible without requiring hover.
